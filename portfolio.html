Thermal Design

Thermal Design - Load Calculation and Design Optimization

This project focuses on heating and cooling load analysis by applying principles of thermodynamics, psychrometric chart and air treatment within an air handling unit (AHU). I evaluated the AHU system in the Rexburg Temple by calculating the thermal loads for both the existing design, which utilizes a steam humidifier, and a proposed design incorporating a pressurized humidifier. Through a comparative analysis of energy consumption and operational costs, I identified the pressurized humidifier as a more efficient alternative. Based on these findings, I recommended replacing the steam humidifier to improve overall system performance and energy efficiency.

Numerical Method

Numerical Method - Root Finding Method Comparison

This project aims to evaluate the performance of root-finding methods, including Bisection, Secant, and Ridder's methods. To perform this comparison, the root of the equation, $x \sin(10x) - x = 1$, was determined using each method. Initial guesses of $x = 0.6$ and $x = 1.2$ were used for the calculations.

  • Chose initial guesses to ensure convergence.
